Test Your Stereo Sound — Check Left & Right Audio Channels

The Stereo Test plays a tone through your left channel, right channel, or both so you can confirm your audio setup is wired correctly. Put on your headphones or sit in front of your speakers, click Play Left, and listen — if the sound comes from the right ear, something's crossed.

Stereo Issues?
Common Problems
  • One channel not working
  • Channels reversed (left/right swapped)
  • Uneven volume between channels
  • Mono audio instead of stereo
Solutions
  • Check cable connections
  • Verify audio output settings
  • Update audio drivers
  • Check system balance settings

Use the tone generator to play specific test frequencies through your speakers. Run a headphone test to verify both channels, or check low-frequency response with the bass test.
