Fix Audio Issues — Microphone & Speaker Troubleshooting Guide

This audio troubleshooting guide walks you through fixes for the most common microphone and speaker problems — mic not detected, no audio input, browser permission errors, distorted sound, and more. Pick the issue category that matches your problem and work through the step-by-step checklist; each section tells you exactly what to check and what to change on your device. If you've already run the mic test online and confirmed your hardware is working, start here to pinpoint whether the issue is in your browser settings, OS permissions, or audio drivers.

Select Your Issue

Microphone Not Working

Microphone Not Working - Solutions

  1. Check Physical Connection — Ensure your microphone is properly plugged in. For USB mics, try a different port. For wireless, check battery.
  2. Check Browser Permissions — Click the lock icon in your browser's address bar → Site Settings → Allow Microphone access.
  3. Check System Settings — Windows: Settings → Privacy → Microphone → Enable access
    Mac: System Preferences → Security & Privacy → Microphone
  4. Select Correct Input Device — In your system sound settings, ensure the correct microphone is selected as the default input device. View detected devices.
  5. Update Audio Drivers — Outdated drivers can cause issues. Check your device manufacturer's website for updates.
No Sound from Speakers

No Sound from Speakers - Solutions

  1. Check Volume Levels — Check system volume, browser volume, and physical speaker/headphone volume controls.
  2. Check Output Device — Ensure correct output device is selected. Check speaker details.
  3. Test with Different Audio — Try playing audio from another website or application to isolate the issue. Use our speaker test.
  4. Restart Audio Service — Windows: services.msc → Windows Audio → Restart
    Mac: sudo killall coreaudiod
Browser Permission Denied

Browser Permission Denied - Solutions

  1. Reset Site Permissions — Click the lock/info icon in your address bar → Reset permissions → Reload the page.
  2. Chrome Settings — Go to chrome://settings/content/microphone → Remove this site from blocked list → Reload.
  3. System-Level Permissions — Windows: Settings → Privacy → Microphone → Allow apps to access
    Mac: System Preferences → Security & Privacy → Microphone → Enable for your browser
Echo or Feedback

Echo or Feedback - Solutions

  1. Use Headphones — The most effective solution. Prevents speaker audio from being picked up by the mic. Test your headphones.
  2. Lower Speaker Volume — Reduce volume to minimize audio reaching the microphone.
  3. Increase Distance — Move your microphone away from speakers.
  4. Enable Echo Cancellation — Check your app/software settings for echo cancellation options. Test for echo.
Low Volume / Quiet Audio

Low Volume - Solutions

  1. Check All Volume Controls — System volume, app volume, browser volume, and physical device volume.
  2. Boost Microphone Levels — Windows: Sound settings → Recording → Properties → Levels → Increase Microphone Boost
    Mac: System Preferences → Sound → Input → Increase input volume
  3. Position Microphone Closer — Move closer to your microphone, typically 6-12 inches for best results. Check audio levels.
Distorted or Crackling Sound

Distorted Sound - Solutions

  1. Lower Input Gain — Audio clipping causes distortion. Reduce microphone gain/boost in system settings.
  2. Check Cable Connections — Loose or damaged cables cause crackling. Try different cables.
  3. Reduce Interference — Move away from electronic devices, routers, and phones that may cause interference. Analyze background noise.
Wrong Device Selected

Wrong Device Selected - Solutions

  1. Check System Sound Settings — Open system sound settings and verify the correct input/output device is selected as default.
  2. Use Device Selector — Many of our tools have device selectors. View your microphones or view your speakers.
Bluetooth Audio Issues

Bluetooth Audio Issues - Solutions

  1. Re-pair Device — Remove the Bluetooth device and pair it again.
  2. Check Battery — Low battery can cause audio issues. Charge your device.
  3. Reduce Distance — Stay within range of your computer. Walls and obstacles can affect signal.
  4. Check Audio Codec — Some Bluetooth profiles have poor microphone quality. Try using "Hands-Free" profile for calls. Test latency.
