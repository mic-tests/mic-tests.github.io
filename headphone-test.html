Test Your Headphones — Check Left/Right Channels & Audio Quality

Put on your headphones and use the Headphone Test to check each ear individually. Click Play Left and the tone should come from your left ear only — if it comes from the right, your headphones are backwards or your audio output is swapped.

What to Check
  • Both ears work: Sound should be clear in both left and right
  • Correct channels: Left plays in left ear, right in right ear
  • Equal volume: Both sides should be equally loud
  • No distortion: Sound should be clean without crackling
  • Good bass: Check with bass test
