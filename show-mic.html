View Your Microphone Details — Check Mic Specs & Settings

Show My Mic reads your browser's audio devices and lists every microphone your system has connected — device name, sample rate, channel count, and device ID for each one. It's surprisingly useful when you have a built-in mic, a USB mic, and a headset all plugged in and you're not sure which one your browser is actually using.
