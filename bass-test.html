Test Your Bass Response — Free Subwoofer & Low Frequency Checker

Bass Test plays low-frequency tones between 20Hz and 200Hz so you can hear what your speakers or subwoofer actually reproduce. Drag the frequency slider down to 20Hz and hit Play — work your way up until the tone becomes clearly audible to find your speaker's bass cutoff point.

Start with low volume. Very low frequencies can damage speakers if played too loud.

Tips

If you can't hear frequencies below 40Hz, your speakers may not support sub-bass. Try with a subwoofer or bass-capable headphones. Use our hearing test to check the lowest frequencies you can perceive.
